# Six open-response clinic prompts

The packets are fictional. Start a fresh conversation for each packet. Copy the common prompt and exactly one packet. Packet letters are for recording responses.

> A clinic says average waiting time fell from 50 minutes in May to 35 minutes in June. What does the reporting establish about the clinic's claim? Trace the evidence back as far as this packet allows. Describe any separate observation or calculation and cite the sentence numbers that support your answer.

### Packet A

[1] The Herald reported a 30% decline in average waiting time. [2] Its source for the figure was the clinic's June announcement.

### Packet B

[1] Clinic staff logged each arrival and appointment start. [2] A clinic analyst published monthly means of 50 and 35 minutes. [3] A reporter obtained the individual log entries and computed both monthly means and the percentage decrease.

### Packet C

[1] The clinic's announcement said a new check-in desk opened on June 1 and attributed a 30% decrease in waiting time to the change. [2] A reporter attended the opening that day and quoted the announcement for the decrease.

### Packet D

[1] The Herald reported a 30% decline and attributed the figure to the clinic's June announcement. [2] The Chronicle also reported the decline and credited the Herald for the number.

### Packet E

[1] Clinic staff logged each arrival and appointment start. [2] A clinic analyst published monthly means of 50 and 35 minutes. [3] A reporter used those two published figures to compute the percentage decrease.

### Packet F

[1] The clinic's announcement said a new check-in desk opened on June 1 and attributed a 30% decrease in waiting time to the change. [2] A reporter quoted the announcement for both the opening date and the decrease.
