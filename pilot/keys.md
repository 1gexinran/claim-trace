# Open-response keys and worked scorecard

Grade each answer on its own. A passing answer gives the conclusions in the table, points to the supporting sentences, and avoids claims that contradict them. Accept paraphrases and equivalent passage references. The examples below are fictional responses written to illustrate the scoring rule.

| Packet | Required conclusions and evidence | Example response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | The Herald traces its figure to the clinic [1–2]. The reporting offers no separate calculation or observation of waiting times. | “The Herald repeats the clinic's number [1–2]. How the clinic derived it is unspecified.” | Pass |
| D | Both papers trace the number to the same announcement [1–2]. | “Two outlets independently found the same result [1–2].” | Fail. The Chronicle credits the Herald. |
| F | Both reported details originate in the clinic's announcement [1–2]. The reporter independently verifies neither the opening nor its effect. | “The reporter quotes the clinic on both claims [1–2]. This supplies no independent observation of either.” | Pass |
| C | The reporter witnessed the opening and relied on the clinic for the decrease [1–2]. The visit cannot establish that the desk caused the decrease. | “The visit [2] verifies the desk opened. The numerical and causal claims still come from the clinic [1–2]. Watching the opening cannot establish that it caused the decrease.” | Pass |
| E | Clinic staff collected the data [1], the analyst calculated the means [2], and the reporter checked only the arithmetic from those means [3]. | “Staff logged the times [1]. The analyst got the means [2]. The reporter verified the 30% calculation using those means [3].” | Pass |
| B | The reporter recomputed the means and percentage [2–3]. Both calculations still use the clinic staff's observations [1,3]. | “The reporter independently collected visit times to check the clinic's 30% [3].” | Fail. The clinic collected the observations. |

The example earns **4/6 packet passes and 1/3 pair passes**. Both C and F pass. The A/D and E/B pairs each contain one failed packet. Keep this free-response exercise separate from the structured benchmark, whose choices and citation rule make automatic scoring reproducible.
